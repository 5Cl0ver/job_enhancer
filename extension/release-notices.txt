# One-time notices shown in a Job Catcher release's notes.
# Format: <version>: <text>. Only the line for the version being released is used.
0.35.0: New in 0.35.0: remove the old Job Catcher first, then load the new folder and press Connect. You'll need to connect once; later updates keep you signed in.
0.40.0: New in 0.40.0: Apply with Claude. Job Catcher takes a photo of every application page and keeps Submit locked until you say so. Chrome may ask you to allow Job Catcher on all sites again.
0.41.0: New in 0.41.0: follow Apply with Claude from the app. "Show me the screen" photos, and Job Catcher spots the thank-you page so you know an application was really sent.
0.42.0: New in 0.42.0: watch the boxes fill in the app, the résumé made for the job is attached (Workday too), and photos work even when you are on another tab. Chrome may ask to know when the computer is locked: that only explains a missing photo.
0.43.0: New in 0.43.0: on Workday the résumé box is found and your résumé goes in, jobs fill from the résumé picked for the job (whole sentences, bullets kept), each step shows only its own boxes, and the phone never goes in Phone Extension. What Claude answers for you is learned once you approve, so Job Catcher fills it next time.
0.44.0: New in 0.44.0: cover letters. The cover letter you pick for a job (or Claude writes for it) goes into the application's cover-letter box, as a file or as text. Never into the résumé box.
0.45.0: New in 0.45.0: Workday works start to finish. Create Account fills your email (never a password), "Have you worked here before?" is answered from your job history, only real agreements are left for you, Workday jobs show in the panel and are tracked when you submit, and "Not in your profile yet" has Add to profile. From now on Job Catcher tells you when it's out of date: NEW on its icon, a card in the panel, a banner in the app.
0.46.0: New in 0.46.0: Apply with Claude lists every box Claude fills, step by step, with the real choices of each dropdown, so a change you tap offers them.
0.47.0: New in 0.47.0: Report a problem straight from Job Catcher. If you run Job Enhancer, a "Report a problem here" link under This page sends what happened, the page (without its search or sign-in codes) and, if you tick it, a picture of the page to Bugs & ideas. Job Catcher also tells the app when its own save, résumé or fill fails, with no page text or personal details.
